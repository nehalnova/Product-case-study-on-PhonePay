Walkthrough

1

Home shows a pending transaction highlighted in coral — flagged, not hidden, the moment you might worry about it.

2

Tap it. Instead of a spinner, the Live Money Trail shows exactly which of 4 stages the payment has reached.

3

Tap the refund guarantee banner to see the countdown timer and plain-language protection promise.

4

Tap "Dispute this transaction" — everything is pre-filled from data PhonePe already has.

5

Submit and see the confirmation — a dispute filed in under 20 seconds, not a multi-step form.

Prototype for portfolio demonstration. Timings and UTR are illustrative. The point being demonstrated is the transparency and speed pattern, not real PhonePe data.
Built as the working prototype referenced in the PhonePe case study — Solution 1: The Live Money Trail.
